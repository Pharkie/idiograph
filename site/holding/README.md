Idiograph's site on idiograph.app from the week `Pharkie/idiograph` goes public as a Preview (Adam, 10 November 2026), two pages, `index.html` and `features.html`, sharing `style.css`. Before then it was a holding page with one line.

Both pages share one header (Adam, 11 November 2026): at the top right, Features and Download, Download jumping to the download on the same page. On `features.html` the small lockup at the left goes home and Features is marked as the page you are on. The home page has no small lockup, since its own large one is below.

The home page, from the top: the lockup with its Preview Edition stamp, coming in one of Home's ways in, then the launch film (`idiograph-film.mp4`, v16, with `film-poster.jpg` as its poster) with controls, never playing by itself, then one paragraph saying what Idiograph is and who it is for, then the Preview Edition's warning, Home's box word for word (`page.preview_note` in `family/static/i18n/en/page.json`, with its warning sign, border, ground and type as `.pe-note` and `.pe-warn` draw it in the app, Adam, 10 November 2026: the Preview is not ready for real work), and then the download, with Features first in the row under it. When the note's words change in the catalogue, copy them here again, on both pages.

`features.html` (Adam, 11 November 2026, the drawn layout C) says what Idiograph does, and what it does not do yet, for a researcher deciding whether it does what they need. Each group is a checklist: a feature's name, a tick, Experimental, Through an agent or Not yet, and its one line, the row opening to its detail. The six most asked come first as a strip of pictures, each opening its row. A find box filters the rows as you type, and a row kept for its detail alone shows the detail's sentence that matched. Below the list come the Preview's warning and the download, as on the home page. The words are from an inventory of the app checked against its code on 11 November 2026: when a feature lands or changes, change its row here by hand. The pictures are in `features/`, WebP at 560 by 350 (the strip's widest place, at twice its size), a light and a dark one each, from the demo and an invented study. The workbook in the strip is drawn in the page, from the demo workbook's own sheet names and columns. `features.js` runs the find and opens a row a link names. Without scripts the find box is hidden and every row is shown and opens by itself.

`get.js` names the visitor's computer from the browser and offers its file from the latest release on GitHub: "Download for Mac" (`Idiograph.dmg`, Apple Silicon), "Download for Windows" (`Idiograph-Setup.exe`), "Download for Linux" (`Idiograph-amd64.deb`, with "Other Linux: AppImage" beside it). An Intel Mac, a phone or tablet, or a computer it cannot tell gets a plain line instead. Below it are always Other downloads (the latest release's page) and Source on GitHub. Without scripts the plain line shows.

`mark.js` is Home's mark code from `family/static/shell.js` (from "the mark coming in on Idiograph's Home" to the end of `homeMarkScene`, at 4278b1d) with its comments left out, as no served file carries a comment. Copy it again from there when Home's ways change. The film is not in git (the repository ignores `*.mp4`): before each deploy it is copied here from `~/dev/idiograph-films/launch/v16-remotion/idiograph-launch-v16.mp4` with its index moved to the front, frames and sound unchanged, by `ffmpeg -i <that file> -map 0 -c copy -movflags +faststart idiograph-film.mp4`. It is 9.8 MB, under the Worker's 25 MB a file.

The pages keep nothing on the visitor's machine, set no cookie, count no one, and ask nothing of any other site: their fonts are the computer's own and their scripts and pictures are served from this folder. Light or dark follows the visitor's setting. They work from a phone's 360 pixels to a wide screen, and with the keyboard alone. Search engines are asked not to list either page (`noindex`).

It is served by a Cloudflare Worker with static assets on idiograph.app and www.idiograph.app, the same pages on both (`wrangler.jsonc` here), deployed by the coordinator with Wrangler on Adam's yes.
