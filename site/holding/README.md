Idiograph's page on idiograph.app from the week `Pharkie/idiograph` goes public as a Preview (Adam, 10 November 2026). Before then it was a holding page with one line. From the top come the lockup with its Preview Edition stamp, coming in one of Home's ways in, then the Preview Edition's warning, Home's box word for word (`page.preview_note` in `family/static/i18n/en/page.json`, with its warning sign, border, ground and type as `.pe-note` and `.pe-warn` draw it in the app, Adam, 10 November 2026: the Preview is not ready for real work), then the launch film (`idiograph-film.mp4`, v16, with `film-poster.jpg` as its poster) with controls, never playing by itself, then one paragraph saying what Idiograph is and who it is for, and then the download. When the note's words change in the catalogue, copy them here again.

`get.js` names the visitor's computer from the browser and offers its file from the latest release on GitHub: "Download for Mac" (`Idiograph.dmg`, Apple Silicon), "Download for Windows" (`Idiograph-Setup.exe`), "Download for Linux" (`Idiograph-amd64.deb`, with "Other Linux: AppImage" beside it). An Intel Mac, a phone or tablet, or a computer it cannot tell gets a plain line instead. Below it are always Other downloads (the latest release's page) and Source on GitHub. Without scripts the plain line shows.

`mark.js` is Home's mark code from `family/static/shell.js` (from "the mark coming in on Idiograph's Home" to the end of `homeMarkScene`, at 4278b1d) with its comments left out, as no served file carries a comment. Copy it again from there when Home's ways change. The film is not in git (the repository ignores `*.mp4`): before each deploy it is copied here from `~/dev/idiograph-films/launch/v16-remotion/idiograph-launch-v16.mp4` with its index moved to the front, frames and sound unchanged, by `ffmpeg -i <that file> -map 0 -c copy -movflags +faststart idiograph-film.mp4`. It is 9.8 MB, under the Worker's 25 MB a file.

The page keeps nothing on the visitor's machine, sets no cookie, counts no one, and asks nothing of any other site: its fonts are the computer's own and its scripts are served from this folder. Light or dark follows the visitor's setting. It works from a phone's 360 pixels to a wide screen.

It is served by a Cloudflare Worker with static assets on idiograph.app and www.idiograph.app, the same page on both (`wrangler.jsonc` here), deployed by the coordinator with Wrangler on Adam's yes.
