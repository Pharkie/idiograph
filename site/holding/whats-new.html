What's new

Idiograph 0.9.32

19 November 2026

  • Home and each project's page now show how far each participant has got, as rings round their picture.
  • You can archive a project you have finished with, and it moves to its own section on Home.
  • When Idiograph brings a newer version of a demo, Home tells you, and puts it in place only when you ask.
  • Working together no longer needs a relay code: anyone with Idiograph can invite others through the relay. Everyone on a team needs this version.
  • Trajectories can be marked complete, as PETs and GETs can.
  • The manual is new: a short guide for when you are stuck, also on the website.
  • A reworded experiential statement now changes on every board at once.
  • In reflexive thematic analysis, a change to an extract's codes now moves it on the Themes board at once.

Earlier versions

Idiograph 0.9.3118 November 2026
  • A Longitudinal IPA project now has at least two timepoints.
  • Share a copy now tells you when it has saved the copy, and can show you the file.
Idiograph 0.9.3017 November 2026
  • A new demo project, Working from home, shows reflexive thematic analysis with its own interviews and recordings.
  • Project settings has a place to keep your reflexivity statement, and your AI agent can read and set it.
  • Each project now keeps its own interviewer name, statement nudges and rounds settings. In a new project the nudges start off.
  • The Code list now has its own Guidance. It follows Braun and Clarke's reflexive thematic analysis.
  • When you make a new project, you choose IPA, Longitudinal IPA or reflexive thematic analysis. You then add transcripts on the project's page. The page puts the work across participants at the top.
  • When you undo your AI agent's run, it now also removes the transcripts and recordings the agent added.
