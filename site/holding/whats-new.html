What's new

Idiograph 0.9.30

17 November 2026

  • A new demo project, Working from home, shows reflexive thematic analysis with its own interviews and recordings.
  • Project settings has a place to keep your reflexivity statement, and your AI agent can read and set it.
  • Each project now keeps its own interviewer name, statement nudges and rounds settings. In a new project the nudges start off.
  • The Code list now has its own Guidance. It follows Braun and Clarke's reflexive thematic analysis.
  • When you make a new project, you choose IPA, Longitudinal IPA or reflexive thematic analysis. You then add transcripts on the project's page. The page puts the work across participants at the top.
  • When you undo your AI agent's run, it now also removes the transcripts and recordings the agent added.

Earlier versions

Idiograph 0.9.2917 November 2026
  • Boards and LGETs save and open much faster in large projects: each board's History is now kept in a folder beside its file.
  • An LPET now draws on PETs: each PET shows which LPETs draw on it, you can note why none does, one PET can inform two LPETs, and the LPET table sets the timepoints side by side, in the app and as a Word or PDF appendix.
  • In Review and replace you decide each find of a name in its sentence, adding transcripts or importing opens the same review, and your AI agent replaces only names written with a capital and leaves any you have unticked.
  • Trajectories have Undo and Redo and many more icons, and LPETs, LGETs and Trajectories each have their own Guidance.
  • Exporting, undoing, deleting and changes made elsewhere now work the same way on every screen.
  • Your AI agent can set cards aside on GETs, LPETs and LGETs boards and put them back, and can shift a transcript's timecodes.
  • A security update to the part of the app that downloads and installs updates.
Idiograph 0.9.2816 November 2026
  • A longitudinal IPA demo: three participants at three timepoints, with LPETs and trajectories.
  • Export sits in the same place on every board, and Trajectories, a participant's annotated transcript and the code list now print and save as PDF and Word.
  • Project history's By an agent shows each agent's changes by run, and you can undo a whole run or one change; Home says when agents have made changes since you last looked.
  • A name on your list of names to replace can be marked to review each time, or left alone inside phrases you give, and your AI agent can add names to the list when you ask.
  • Your AI agent can now start your GETs or LGETs, mark passages that need no statement, bring new statements into your LPETs, and show what bringing in a timepoint's changes would do before it does it.
