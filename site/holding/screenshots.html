Screenshots

The clips use the app's demo, made with AI: they show the app, not how to do IPA.

Transcripts in the shapes you have

Bring in a transcript from Teams or Zoom (VTT), SRT, plain text, Word (.docx), CSV or Excel, and it becomes the participant's workbook.

IPA's own steps

Three screens follow Smith, Flowers and Larkin's (2022) steps: experiential statements, then PETs for each participant, then GETs across them.

Longitudinal IPA

Interview each participant more than once and keep each timepoint's statements and PETs apart, as longitudinal IPA does (Farr and Nizza 2019).

Tables for your supervisor and thesis

Print a participant's table of PETs, save it as a PDF, or write it as a Microsoft Word document in the book's layout.

PETs on a canvas

Drag a participant's statements into PETs, sub-PETs and groups on a canvas, as you would with cards on a table.

Your Excel workbook, on your computer

Each participant's transcript, notes, statements and quotes are kept in an Excel workbook in a folder on your computer.
