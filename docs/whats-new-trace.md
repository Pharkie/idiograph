# What's new in Trace

Newest first. Each version's notes are what the updater shows, eight lines at most, and
`packaging/release.py` reads them from here.

## 1.0.0, 21 October 2026

Trace is an app of its own, with its own window, icon and installer, where it was a part of Idiograph.
Your Trace projects, their manuscripts and claims stay where they were, and open from Trace's Home.
Its settings are its own after its first start, which copies what it shares from Idiograph's, Appearance and the AI features among them.
Its Home ends with Idiograph and Sift, each with Open when it is installed or Download when not.
Trace is now free and open source, under the GNU AGPL v3.
