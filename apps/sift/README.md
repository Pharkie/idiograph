# Sift's own code

Sift's own modules and its `static/` files, since the family codebase's third build (22 October 2026), in an import root that `boot.setup` puts on `sys.path` when the run serves Sift: `sift.py`, a screening, `sift_search.py`, a review's searches, `sift_second.py`, the second screener, `sift_ai.py`, its AI proposals, `sift_oa.py`, Find open-access PDFs, `sift_zotero.py`, the sync to Zotero, `make_world.py`, the map's maker, `sift_routes.py`, `sift_cli.py`, `sift_mcp.py` and `sift_find.py`, its routes, command, tools and part of Search, each registered with the family's module as it is imported (`boot.load`), `sift_main.py`, its entry, `sift_words.py`, its words for a person from the catalogue (`static/i18n/`), and `static/`, its screens, their styles, its icon, the map, Mermaid, its parts of the family's page, `sift.html`, and its parts of the shell, `sift_settings.js`. Its table sorts, filters and draws its groups through the family's (`family/static/table.js`), reading its records through its own reader, `sfRead`. A module here imports the family's (`family/`) and Sift's own, never another app's. See docs/internals.md, "The family's folders".
