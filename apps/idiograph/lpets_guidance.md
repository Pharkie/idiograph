# LPETs guidance

The lines the LPETs screen shows in the board panel under Guidance, in the shape of `themes_guidance.md` beside this file.
How to follow one aspect of a person's experience across the timepoints. Grounded in Farr and Nizza (2019), Nizza (2022), Spiers (thesis), Smith (2024), Smith et al. (2025) and
Smith and Nizza (2022). Each heading is a small group in the panel and each line under it starts with `- `. The server reads
the file afresh on every open, so edit it here and it changes in the app on the next reload. Keep every line short
enough to take in at a glance, and the whole short enough to read in a minute.

## What an LPET is

- An LPET is your interpretation of one aspect of this person's experience across time, given as a state at each timepoint.
- The cards under it show what it draws on. It is not their sum: your reading of the whole case across time shapes it too.
- It can show change, continuity or both. A thread that stays stuck or gets worse is as valid as one that improves.

## How to build them

- Lay the timepoints' PETs side by side and read across them for what changes and what holds.
- Hold the whole case in mind as you do: what are the main things happening for this person across the study?
- Under each LPET, place what it draws on at each timepoint: a whole PET, a sub-theme, or a single statement where the thread runs across several PETs.
- Say in the LPET's note what it draws on beyond its cards.
- Not every PET carries through. Leave the rest in Unplaced, and be able to say why.
- Where the PETs are thin, go back to the transcript.

## Each timepoint

- Give the LPET a state at each timepoint in a few words, and one quote that shows it.
- Keep each quote to its own timepoint: said then, not looked back on later.

## Before you finish

- Can each LPET be traced back to what it draws on at every timepoint?
- Does each LPET hold something at every timepoint, or have a reason it doesn't?
- Is each Unplaced PET there for a reason you could give?
