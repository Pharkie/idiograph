# LGETs guidance

The lines the LGETs screen shows in the board panel under Guidance, in the shape of `themes_guidance.md` beside this file.
How to bring people's LPETs together over time. Grounded in Farr and Nizza (2019), Nizza (2022), Spiers (thesis), Smith (2024), Smith et al. (2025) and
Smith and Nizza (2022). Each heading is a small group in the panel and each line under it starts with `- `. The server reads
the file afresh on every open, so edit it here and it changes in the app on the next reload. Keep every line short
enough to take in at a glance, and the whole short enough to read in a minute.

## What an LGET is

- An LGET is your interpretation of a pattern over time that several participants share.
- The LPETs under it show what it draws on, not a sum of them.
- Each person stays visible inside it: show where they meet and where they differ.

## How to build them

- Finish each person's LPETs first, then work across people.
- Group by what happens over time, not by topic alone. A shared trajectory, positive, mixed or negative, can hold an LGET.
- An unresolved or negative trajectory is a finding, never a remainder.

## Before you finish

- Is every participant here, and is each LPET left out for a reason?
- Read each LPET in its LGET against its person: does the group story still hold true to them?
