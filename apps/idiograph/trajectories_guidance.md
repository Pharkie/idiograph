# Trajectories guidance

The lines the Trajectories screen shows in the board panel under Guidance, in the shape of `themes_guidance.md` beside this file.
How to read and fill the rows. Grounded in Farr and Nizza (2019), Nizza (2022), Spiers (thesis), Smith (2024), Smith et al. (2025) and
Smith and Nizza (2022). Each heading is a small group in the panel and each line under it starts with `- `. The server reads
the file afresh on every open, so edit it here and it changes in the app on the next reload. Keep every line short
enough to take in at a glance, and the whole short enough to read in a minute.

## What it shows

- One row for each LPET: its state at each timepoint, a key quote for each, and the person's overall trajectory.

## Key quotes

- Choose the quote that best shows the state at that timepoint, from what was said at that timepoint.
- Prefer a quote from the LPET's own cards. One from elsewhere in the interview must still show that state.
- Each quote carries its participant, timepoint and time, so a reader can check it.

## States

- Write each state in your own words, short enough to read across the row.

## Before you finish

- Read each row from left to right: does it tell this person's movement in one breath?
